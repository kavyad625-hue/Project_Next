Partnr: working prototype

Concept prototype for the Partnr × ProdMan case competition. Not a real app; prices and data are illustrative.

Try this

  • Pick Hinglish or हिन्दी. The app talks back in that language (turn sound on).
  • Tap Bolo and say: "Splendor ke brake shoe do set aur 20W40 ke chaar dabbe, Pulsar ki clutch plate". Use Chrome and allow the mic.
  • Tap the camera → Parchi tab → capture. The AI reads the parchi into a cart.
  • A part not in the dark store goes to the anonymous Partnr Network.
  • Watch for Swiggy-style notifications on the home screen, or trigger them from Profile.
Voice uses your browser's speech recognition (Chrome works best). Parchi and part reading from photos is simulated for the demo; you can edit the detected text to test the Hinglish parser.
